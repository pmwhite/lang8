How to play

Controls

  • Steer with the left panel, or ← and →.
  • Hold the trick panel (or Space) to crouch. Drag, or tap arrows, to add flips and spins. Release to jump.
  • Hold again in the air to grab.
  • Land on a rail or box, or ride onto its end, to grind.

Timing

  • Bigger tricks need more air. You get the most air by releasing at the lip of a ramp.
  • While you hold, ramps glow green where releasing will land the trick. A green ring around you means release now.
  • The tag over each jump shows what the trick would score there, or TOO BIG.
  • Without enough air the landing is sketchy and scores nothing.

Courses and parks

  • A downhill run ends at the finish line.
  • A park run lasts 60 seconds. Ride up the walls to jump off them.
  • There is a new daily course and daily park every day.

Groups

  • Join a group with its invite link. Your best scores post to it automatically.
  • Tap ▶ next to a score to watch that run.
