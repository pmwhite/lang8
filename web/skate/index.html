How to play

Controls

  • Hold your phone in both hands. Your left thumb steers (or ← and →).
  • Hold your right thumb down (or Space) to crouch. Slide it, or tap arrows, to add flips and spins. Let go to jump.
  • Hold again in the air to grab.
  • Land on a rail or box, or ride onto its end, to grind.

Timing

  • Bigger tricks need more air. You get the most air by letting go at the lip of a ramp.
  • While you hold, ramps glow green where letting go will land the trick. A green ring around you means let go now.
  • The tag over each jump shows what the trick would score there, or TOO BIG.
  • Without enough air the landing is sketchy and scores nothing.

Scoring

Flips1: 600 · 2: 900 · 3: 1100
Spins180: 200 · 360: 350 · 540: 480 · 720: 580 · 900: 660 · 1080: 720
Grab300 to 500, more the longer you hold
Grind300, plus 150 a second

Where you jump from favors one thing:

KickerFlips ×2
Halfpipe or bowl wallSpins ×2
CliffGrabs ×2.5
GapFlip and spin together ×1.5

Then the whole trick is multiplied:

Air time×0.5 to ×1.6
Switch (riding backwards)×1.5
Flat (not off a ramp)×0.25
Repeat×0.5 each time

Landing short of a gap scores nothing.

Courses and parks

  • A course run ends at the finish line.
  • A park run lasts 60 seconds. Ride up the walls to jump off them.
  • There is a new daily course and daily park every day.

Leaderboards

  • Leaderboards are by invitation. Open your personal link once, and this device plays as you.
  • Every run you finish on a course, park or daily is posted for the people you play with.
  • Tap a name to see that player, or ▶ to watch a run.
