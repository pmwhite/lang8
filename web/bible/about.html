About Smooth Bible

Smooth Bible is a free Bible reader for the iPhone and the web. These are the sources it reads from and the work it rests on.

Changes to the data

  • STEP Bible’s HTML was converted to plain styled text: bold, italic, paragraphs and references kept; other markup dropped, and entities decoded.
  • Entries were grouped by base Strong’s number, the sense matching the word’s part of speech first.
  • Hebrew cantillation and paragraph marks were removed for display; vowel points kept.
  • Psalm titles were mapped to verse 1, as the BSB’s tables have them.
  • Transliterations of Hebrew lemmas are the tables’ own, or made in their style.
  • Cross references to 3 John 1:15 are given at 1:14, as the BSB numbers it.
  • The commentary’s markup was converted to plain styled text.
  • Each word’s sense is STEP’s disambiguated Strong’s number in TAHOT and TAGNT, matched to the BSB’s words of the verse by base number, in the original order.
  • MACULA’s Proximity pairs were reduced to base Strong’s numbers (Aramaic as Hebrew), each word’s ten nearest kept.
  • Recordings are played as published. Verse timings for them were computed by forced alignment.
