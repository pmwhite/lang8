recipe shakshuka Shakshuka with feta
meal breakfast
serves 2
minutes 30
about Eggs poached in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce.
use olive_oil 15 1 tbsp olive oil
use onion 110 1 onion, sliced
use red_pepper 120 1 red pepper, sliced
use garlic 9 3 cloves garlic, sliced
use cumin 2 1 tsp ground cumin
use paprika 2 1 tsp smoked paprika
use tinned_tomato 400 1 tin (400 g) tomatoes
use egg 200 4 eggs
extra feta 50 50 g feta, crumbled
extra parsley 10 1 handful parsley, chopped
staple salt and pepper
step Warm the oil in a wide pan over a medium heat. Add the onion and pepper with a pinch of salt and cook until soft and sweet, about 10 minutes.
step Stir in the garlic, cumin and paprika and cook for a minute, until fragrant.
step Tip in the tomatoes, season, and simmer for 8 to 10 minutes until thick enough to hold a hollow.
step Make four hollows and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook gently for 5 to 7 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks still soft.
step Scatter with the feta and parsley and eat from the pan.

recipe meatballs Turkey meatballs with courgette
meal dinner
serves 3
minutes 40
about Tender herby meatballs in tomato sauce over courgette ribbons.
use turkey_mince 500 500 g turkey mince
use egg 50 1 egg
use parmesan 30 30 g Parmesan, grated
use parsley 20 1 handful parsley, chopped
use garlic 9 3 cloves garlic, grated
use olive_oil 15 1 tbsp olive oil
use tinned_tomato 400 1 tin tomatoes
use oregano 1 1 tsp dried oregano
use zucchini 600 3 courgettes, in ribbons
staple salt and pepper
step Mix the turkey, egg, half the Parmesan, the parsley, a third of the garlic and a good pinch of salt. Roll into 18 balls.
step Brown the meatballs in the oil, in batches, then set aside. In the same pan cook the rest of the garlic for a minute, add the tomatoes and oregano, and simmer for 10 minutes.
step Return the meatballs and simmer, covered, for 12 minutes, until cooked through.
step Stir the courgette ribbons into a hot pan for 2 minutes with a pinch of salt. Serve the meatballs over them, with the rest of the Parmesan.
