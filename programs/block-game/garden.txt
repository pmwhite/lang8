# Moonlit garden: a world for the block game.
#
# Each level teaches something, in order:
#   Garden Gate: push a block out of the way
#   Two Abreast: pushes do not chain; get behind a block
#   Steps: ramps climb onto walls, and wall tops are paths
#   Topple: from a ramp, walking into a stack knocks its top off
#   Ramparts: a knocked-off block is a step up onto the walls
#   Stepping Stone: a block pushed into water becomes a stepping stone
#   Ford: blocks can be pushed across stepping stones
#   Shallows: you can wade, push things along the bottom, and climb out on a ramp
#   Hitch: a ramp rides the block it rests on, even uphill, and lands on it in water
#   Twins: linked shapes move together
#   Ferry: a sunk twin slides along the bottom with its partner
#   Anchor: a sunk twin only lets its partner move where it can follow
#   Treetops: tree crowns hold you up; carts and ramps reach them
#
# Every level was checked with solve.l8: solvable, and no way out but its doors.
