Smooth Bible

Smooth Bible is a free Bible reader for iPhone. The text runs in one continuous scroll from Genesis to Revelation, with poetry set in lines, section headings and footnotes. The Berean Standard Bible, the King James Version, the World English Bible, the American Standard Version and Young’s Literal Translation are included, and the English Standard Version can be read online. The page can be set in different themes, typefaces and sizes, and the app can read the text aloud.

Verses can be highlighted in colour, bookmarked and given notes, and any verse can be compared across the versions. For study, the Hebrew and Greek words are shown under each verse, and each word has a page with its meanings and grammar in plain English and the other places it is used. Cross references, Matthew Henry’s Concise Commentary and reading plans are also included.
