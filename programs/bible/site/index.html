Smooth Bible

A Bible reader for iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store.

Smooth Bible is a free app for reading the Bible on an iPhone. The main text is the Berean Standard Bible. The King James, World English, American Standard and Young’s Literal versions are included as well, and the ESV can be read online. The whole Bible scrolls continuously from Genesis to Revelation, with a choice of themes, typefaces and text sizes, and the app can read the text aloud.

Any verse can be highlighted, bookmarked or given a note, and notes can also be written on a whole chapter. They are collected in a library that can be searched and exported as a Markdown file. There are reading plans and an optional verse of the day.

For any verse, the app can show the Hebrew or Greek underneath the BSB, word by word. Each word shows its dictionary form, meaning, Strong’s number and parsing, and a concordance of where else it is used and how it is translated. A verse can be compared across all the versions, and has cross references and Matthew Henry’s commentary beside it.

Smooth Bible needs iOS 16 or later.
