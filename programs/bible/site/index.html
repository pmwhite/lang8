Smooth Bible

A Bible reader for iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store.

Smooth Bible is a free Bible reader for iPhone, made for reading the whole Bible straight through and for studying it closely.

Features

  • The Berean Standard Bible, with the King James, World English, American Standard and Young’s Literal versions
  • The English Standard Version, online
  • One continuous scroll from Genesis to Revelation
  • Poetry set in lines, with section headings, footnotes and the words of Jesus in red if you like
  • Six themes and five typefaces, with any text size
  • Listen, which reads aloud from any verse and keeps going
  • Go to any book, chapter or verse, with a history of where you’ve been
  • Search the whole Bible
  • Highlights in five colours
  • Bookmarks
  • Notes on verses and on whole chapters
  • A library of your highlights, bookmarks and notes, with search, sorting and export
  • Compare a verse across every version
  • The Hebrew and Greek under every verse, word by word
  • Word study: dictionary form, meaning, Strong’s number and grammar in plain English
  • A concordance of every place a word is used, and how it’s translated
  • Cross references, with a preview of each passage
  • Matthew Henry’s Concise Commentary
  • Reading plans
  • A verse of the day
  • Share verses as text or as an image
  • Keeps the screen awake while you read, if you like
