Smooth Bible

The Bible on your iPhone, as calm as a printed page.

Coming soon to the App Store. Free, with no ads, accounts or tracking.

  • Reading firstThe whole Bible in one continuous scroll, set like a well-made book, with a bar that gets out of the way. Six themes, five typefaces, and Listen when you'd rather hear it.
  • Your notes, keptHighlights, bookmarks, and notes on verses or whole chapters, all in one library you can search and export.
  • The words underneathTap a verse to see its Hebrew or Greek word by word: what each word means, its grammar in plain English, and everywhere else it appears.
  • Versions to compareThe Berean Standard Bible, with the King James, World English, American Standard and Young's Literal alongside, and the ESV online.
  • Yours aloneEverything you write stays on your iPhone. There is no account to make and nothing to sign in to.
