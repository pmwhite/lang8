Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Data in the app

Highlights, bookmarks, notes, reading history, reading plans and settings are stored on the iPhone. They are not sent to Smooth Bible or to anyone else. The app has no accounts, analytics or advertising.

The Bible texts, the Hebrew and Greek data and the commentary are part of the app. Reading-plan reminders are scheduled on the device.

The ESV

When the English Standard Version is read or searched, the app fetches the passage or search results over the internet through smoothbible.com. These requests are not logged or kept.

Listening

Recordings are streamed directly from OpenBible.com (the Berean Standard Bible), eBible.org (the World English Bible) and audio.esv.org (the ESV). Those servers receive the device’s internet address and which chapter’s recording is asked for, as with any web request, under their own policies. The app sends them nothing else.

For the ESV, the app first asks smoothbible.com for the recording’s address. That request carries the book and chapter, as for the text, and only counts of such requests are kept.

Downloaded and cached recordings stay on the iPhone. The iPhone’s own voice, used for the other versions and whenever a recording can’t be reached, works on the device.

This website
