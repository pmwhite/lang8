Privacy

Smooth Bible collects nothing about you.

In the app

Your highlights, bookmarks, notes, reading history, reading plans and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or anyone else. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

The Bible texts, the Hebrew and Greek study data and the commentary are part of the app, so reading them needs no connection. Listen uses your iPhone's own voices. Reminders such as the verse of the day are scheduled on your iPhone.

The ESV

The English Standard Version can't be stored in the app, so when you read it the app asks our server at smoothbible.com for the chapter, which fetches it from Crossway's ESV API. The request contains the book and chapter (or, if you choose to search the ESV, your search words), and like any internet request it carries your network address.

Our server keeps none of it. It writes no logs of what is asked for. It holds your address for one minute, only to stop any single device from using up the shared daily allowance, and it keeps a count of total requests with nothing that identifies anyone. The server runs on Cloudflare, which handles the connection; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. Crossway sees requests coming from our server, not from you.

If you never choose the ESV, the app makes no requests to anyone, except an occasional small check of whether the ESV is available, which carries nothing but the question.
