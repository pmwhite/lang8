Contact

Email contact@smoothbible.com. For a problem with the app, it helps to include the iPhone model, the iOS version and the app’s version, which is at the bottom of Reading settings → Acknowledgements.

Notes

Notes, highlights and bookmarks are kept on the iPhone. To keep a copy elsewhere, use Library → Notes → Export, which shares them all as a Markdown file.

The ESV

Crossway’s terms let the app show the ESV but not store it, so ESV chapters are downloaded as they are read and need a connection. All readers share a daily allowance from Crossway; if it runs out, the ESV is available again the next day. The other versions are in the app and work offline.
