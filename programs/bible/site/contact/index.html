Contact

Questions, problems and ideas are all welcome.

Write to contact@smoothbible.com. It helps to mention your iPhone model and iOS version, and the app's version (shown at the bottom of Reading settings → Acknowledgements).

Common questions

Where are my notes kept? Only on your iPhone. To keep a copy elsewhere, use Library → Notes → Export, which shares all your notes and highlights as a Markdown file.

Why does the ESV need a connection? Crossway allows free apps to show the ESV but not to store it, so it is fetched as you read. The other versions are in the app and work anywhere.

Why is the ESV sometimes resting? Every reader of the ESV in Smooth Bible shares one daily allowance from Crossway. If it runs out, the ESV comes back the next day; the BSB is always there.

Will it ever cost anything? No. Smooth Bible is free, with no ads or purchases, and always will be.
