# How it follows them:
# - The skeleton of DESIGN.md §16.4.4 decides the periods and its named placements; within them
#   Townsend's order is followed at the grain of chapters and events, with his placements of
#   psalms and prophets. Samuel, Kings and Chronicles are read side by side: each event's
#   parallels are one block (Townsend reads one and prints the other beside it).
# - Where Townsend interleaves single verses within an event, or moves a few verses a short way,
#   the passages are read whole in their book's order. So, not kept: Genesis 20 after 13:1, and
#   his moves of 25:1-6, 25:12-18, 35:28-29 and 38; Exodus 18 after Numbers 10:10; Numbers 9:1-14
#   after Leviticus 10; Numbers 33's stations read through Exodus and Numbers; 1 Samuel 16:14-23
#   after 18:9 and 17:55-56 before 17:41; 2 Samuel 4:4 with 9; 1 Kings 11:15-20 with 2 Samuel 8;
#   1 Chronicles 13:1-4 with 2 Samuel 5; 1 Chronicles 27:23-24 with the census; Isaiah 38:9-20
#   after Isaiah 34-35 (here Isaiah 38 is read whole with Hezekiah's illness); Jeremiah 29:15
#   after 29:20; and his verse-by-verse weaving of Kings with Chronicles and of Jeremiah 39 with 52.
# - The Gospels are Robertson's 184 sections in order (128 has two parts, a and b), each a block
#   of its parallels read Matthew, Mark, Luke, John (Robertson prints Mark first). Sections 54
#   (the Sermon on the Mount) and 64 (the parables) are one block each, not his subsections.
#   Where a verse is in two sections it is read in the first; Luke 2:52, which he leaves out, is
#   read with 2:51; each is noted on its line. The passages he adds from Acts and 1 Corinthians
#   are read in their own books, so section 182 (1 Corinthians 15:7 alone) has no block.
#
# Where it departs from Townsend (the skeleton's placements, and our own):
# - 1 Chronicles 1-9 before David (Townsend: at the end of the Old Testament, with Nehemiah
#   12:10-26, which here stays in Nehemiah).
# - Judges and Ruth before 1 Samuel, Ruth after Judges (Townsend: Ruth after Judges 6:6, and
#   Samson, Judges 13-16, woven with 1 Samuel 1-4).
# - David's psalms: those the skeleton names at their events; the rest where Townsend places
#   them in David's life after 1 Chronicles 16; those he places earlier (in Saul's time and
#   before the ark came to Jerusalem) or later (in the exile and the return) are read together
#   after 1 Chronicles 16 in the Psalter's order, each noted there.
# - Psalm 90 with Moses at Numbers 14, and Psalm 88 at Exodus 2, are Townsend's places (the
#   skeleton lists Psalm 90 after Deuteronomy). The Asaph and Korah psalms of the kingdom are at
#   Townsend's places in it (42-43 and 45 with David, 78 at his end, 47 with the ark brought into
#   the temple, 46 and 82 with Jehoshaphat, 44, 73, 75 and 76 with Hezekiah).
# - Obadiah and Joel early (the skeleton): Obadiah with Jehoram, when Edom revolted, Joel with
#   Joash (Townsend: Obadiah under Ahaz, Joel under Uzziah).
# - Jeroboam II's prophets (Hosea 1-4, Amos, Jonah) before Uzziah's reign and Isaiah 1-6
#   (Townsend reads 2 Chronicles 26 first). Jehoshaphat (2 Chronicles 17-20) after 1 Kings 21, at
#   1 Kings 22 (Townsend: before Elijah); Jehoram of Judah (2 Chronicles 21) after 2 Kings 8:15
#   (Townsend: at 2 Kings 6:23).
# - The fall of Samaria (2 Kings 17, 18:9-12) and Hosea 7-14 before Hezekiah (the skeleton;
#   Townsend reads them after Hezekiah's reign and Isaiah 40-66).
# - Ezekiel and then Daniel after Jeremiah and Lamentations (the skeleton; Townsend weaves
#   Ezekiel 1-31 into Zedekiah's reign and its aftermath, reads Daniel 1:1-7 under Jehoiakim,
#   Daniel 6 with Ezra 1 and Daniel 10-12 after Ezra 4:5); each book in Townsend's order within.
#   Jehoiachin's release (2 Kings 25:27-30, Jeremiah 52:31-34) ends Jeremiah (Townsend: before
#   Daniel 7).
# - The psalms of the exile and the return at Townsend's places (the skeleton's "Psalm 126 and
#   the remaining psalms of the return": every psalm has a place, so none is left for the end).
# - Zechariah 9-14 with Zechariah 1-8 (the skeleton; Townsend: after Ezra 10). Esther whole,
#   then Ezra 7-10 (the skeleton; Townsend reads Ezra 7-8 within Esther 2). Malachi around
#   Nehemiah 13:4-31 is Townsend's.
# - Acts and the letters: the skeleton's places, each noted on its line with Townsend's.
